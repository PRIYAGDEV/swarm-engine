DIGITAL ECOSYSTEM

BMW Curved Display & Operating System 9

A single sculptured, anti-reflective glass surface sweeps across the cockpit, merging the 12.3-inch information cluster and 14.9-inch central control display. Powered by QuickSelect touch access, zero submenu latency, and augmented-reality HUD navigation.

  • Zero-layer menu navigation with customizable live widgets
  • Natural voice interaction with BMW Intelligent Personal Assistant
  • Over-The-Air (OTA) Remote Software Upgrades delivered continuously
eDRIVE PROPULSION

BMW eDrive High-Voltage Architecture

Electrically excited synchronous motors operate without rare-earth magnetic materials, maintaining peak torque across extended RPM bands. The 800V high-voltage architecture unlocks up to 205 kW DC ultra-fast charging—delivering 100 miles of range in just 10 minutes.

  • Cradle-to-grave rare-earth free motor construction
  • Integrated heat pump technology for cold-weather range preservation
  • Intelligent thermal pre-conditioning linked to GPS charging routes
M DYNAMICS

Intelligent M xDrive & Active Differential

An electro-mechanical multi-plate clutch continuously redistributes torque between front and rear axles within milliseconds. Drivers can seamlessly toggle between all-wheel grip (4WD), high-agility track bias (4WD Sport), and pure rear-wheel drive drift mode (2WD).

  • Active M Differential with 0-100% electro-mechanical lock
  • Integrated Braking System with adjustable pedal feedback feel
  • Carbon Core lightweight structural rigidity reinforcement
100+ YEARS OF MOTORSPORT DOMINANCE

HERITAGE FORGED ON THE TRACK

From pioneering aero-engine precision in 1916 to creating the world's most successful touring car, the pursuit of pure driving passion has never ceased.

1916
FOUNDATION

Bayerische Motoren Werke is Born

Engineered out of Munich, Germany, BMW begins crafting high-altitude aircraft engines, instilling an obsession with lightweight metallurgy, power density, and mechanical perfection.

1936
RACING ICON

The Legendary BMW 328 Roadster

The lightweight tubular spaceframe 328 Roadster shocks the automotive world by dominating the grueling 1,000-mile Mille Miglia, establishing BMW's road-racing dominance forever.

1972
BMW MOTORSPORT GMBH

The Birth of the ///M Division

Headed by racing legend Jochen Neerpasch, BMW creates the M Division and the world-famous tricolor livery: Cyan (Bavarian Blue), Dark Blue (Partnership/Texaco), and Red (Pure Racing Passion).

1978
SUPERCAR

BMW M1: The Mid-Engine Masterpiece

Styled by Giorgetto Giugiaro and powered by Paul Rosche's legendary 3.5L M88 engine, the M1 spawns the exhilarating Procar Championship starring Formula 1 legends.

1986
LEGENDARY TOURING CAR

The E30 M3 Revolution

Born strictly for Group A homologation, the E30 M3 racks up more touring car championship victories than any other vehicle in motorsport history, creating the blueprint for all M cars to follow.

2025+
